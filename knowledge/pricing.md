# Pricing & Account Tiers

## Tier Structure

### 1. Free Starter Tier
- **Cost**: $0 / month
- **File Upload Limit**: Up to 10,000 rows per CSV file.
- **Audits & Cleaning**: Standard duplicate detection, blank cell filling, whitespace trimming.
- **AI Conversational Auditor**: Standard Gemini 3.6 Flash queries (up to 50 AI questions/day).
- **Team Collaboration**: Single-user workspace.

### 2. Pro Tier
- **Cost**: $29 / month
- **File Upload Limit**: Up to 250,000 rows per file.
- **Audits & Cleaning**: Advanced anomaly scoring, outlier detection, custom regex validation, schema drift alerts.
- **AI Conversational Auditor**: Unlimited Gemini 3.6 Flash & Gemini 3.1 Pro deep thinking models, image/screenshot analysis.
- **Team Collaboration**: Up to 10 team members with role-based permissions.
- **Integrations**: Integrated Gmail API email dispatch, automated PDF report branding.

### 3. Enterprise Tier
- **Cost**: Custom / Annual
- **File Upload Limit**: Multi-million row datasets (streamed processing).
- **Audits & Cleaning**: Dedicated PostgreSQL database synchronization, custom compliance rulesets, SLA guarantee.
- **AI Conversational Auditor**: Custom fine-tuned knowledge base embeddings, unlimited RAG queries, priority model routing.
- **Team Collaboration**: Unlimited workspace members, SSO / SAML authentication, audit log exports.
