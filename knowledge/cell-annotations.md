# Cell Annotations & Collaborative Review

## Annotation Board
The Cell Annotation Board enables team members to leave targeted feedback and audit notes on specific rows and columns inside uploaded spreadsheets.

## Features
- **Row & Cell Specific Threading**: Tag specific row indexes and column headers (e.g. Row 14, Column `amount`).
- **Severity Flags**: Mark annotations as `Info`, `Warning`, or `Critical Error`.
- **Resolution Tracking**: Toggle annotation status between `Open` and `Resolved` once data issues are remediated.
- **Team Visibility**: All workspace members with Editor, Admin, or Owner roles can view and respond to cell annotations in real time.
